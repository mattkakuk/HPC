## **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

### **Choosing White Without Considering Undertones**

Not all white cabinets are the same. Some whites are warm, some are cool, and
some are bright and crisp.

The wrong undertone can clash with flooring, countertops, or wall colors.

### **Ignoring Lighting**

A cabinet color can look different under natural light, warm bulbs, or cool
LEDs. Lighting should always be considered before final decisions are made.

### **Forgetting About Maintenance**

White cabinets are not difficult to maintain, but they should be chosen with
everyday use in mind. Durable finishes and easy-clean surfaces are important.
