## **Navigating the South Florida Climate: Material Selection**

Living near the ocean comes with incredible perks: sunshine, ocean breezes,
and palm trees! However, Florida's humidity can wreak havoc on cheap
materials. Have you ever seen particle board cabinets swell up like a sponge
after exposure to moisture? It isn't pretty.

### **Solid Wood vs. Engineered Alternatives**

We believe in offering all-wood construction because real timber resists
warping, holds screws tight, and stands up to everyday moisture far better
than cheap particle board.

  * **Solid Hardwood:** Exceptional strength, natural grain, and unbeatable longevity.
  * **Plywood Boxes:** Sturdy, lightweight, and engineered specifically to handle temperature and moisture fluctuations.
  * **MDF (Medium-Density Fiberboard):** Great for painted cabinet doors because it stays smooth without expanding or contracting.

## **Maintenance Tips for Long-Lasting Cabinetry**

Once your stunning new[ **kitchen cabinets in Pompano Beach,
FL**](https://www.google.com/maps?cid=12832526037063468766) are installed,
keeping them looking brand-new is surprisingly simple.

  1. **Clean Spills Immediately:** Wipe away grease Splatters or food residue with a soft microfiber cloth and mild warm water.
  2. **Avoid Harsh Chemicals:** Skip abrasive sponges, ammonia, or bleach products that can dull wood glazes and protective clear coats.
  3. **Control Indoor Humidity:** Keep your air conditioning running steadily during humid Florida summers to preserve wood integrity.
