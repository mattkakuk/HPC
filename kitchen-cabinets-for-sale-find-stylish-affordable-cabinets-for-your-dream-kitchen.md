## **Understanding Cabinet Construction Before You Buy**

An attractive cabinet display doesn't always reveal how well a product will
perform after years of daily use.

That's why we believe understanding cabinet construction is one of the most
important steps in shopping for new cabinetry.

### **Cabinet Boxes and Structural Materials**

Cabinet boxes support shelves, drawers, doors, and the items stored inside
them.

Depending on the product line, manufacturers may use plywood, engineered wood,
or other cabinet-grade materials.

Each option has different characteristics involving cost, weight, durability,
and moisture resistance.

We encourage customers to compare actual construction specifications rather
than assuming all cabinets with similar appearances are built the same way.

### **Drawer Construction and Hardware Quality**

Drawers experience repeated movement throughout the day, making their
construction especially important.

When comparing cabinets, we suggest examining drawer joints, slide mechanisms,
and how smoothly the drawers operate.

Well-designed hardware can make ordinary kitchen activities feel noticeably
more comfortable.

#### **Are Soft-Close Drawers Worth the Investment?**

Soft-close mechanisms help prevent drawers and cabinet doors from slamming.

They can reduce noise and create a smoother closing experience.

For busy households, this feature may be particularly appealing.

We recommend testing soft-close systems when comparing cabinet collections so
you can evaluate the difference firsthand.
