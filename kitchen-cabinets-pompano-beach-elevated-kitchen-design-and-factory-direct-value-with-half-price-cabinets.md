## **Five Costly Cabinet Mistakes We Help You Sidestep**

After guiding thousands of local remodels, we know exactly where DIY layouts
run into trouble. Keep these common missteps off your renovation checklist:

  1. **Ignoring Appliance Door Clearances:** Failing to account for thick refrigerator doors or protruding range handles can prevent adjacent corner drawers from opening fully.
  2. **Leaving Empty Gaps Below Tall Ceilings:** Stopping short wall cabinets below a nine-foot ceiling creates a hard-to-clean dust shelf; extending cabinetry upward with stacked uppers or crown molding adds storage and visual height.
  3. **Skipping Under-Cabinet LED Lighting:** Even the best[ kitchen cabinets pompano beach](https://goo.gl/maps/NU6TK1eTns6xw3Dt7) homes will cast shadows over prep counters without warm task lighting mounted beneath upper frames.
  4. **Assuming Walls and Floors Are Level:** Almost every home has subtle framing variations; proper scribe filler strips ensure doors align cleanly and swing without rubbing drywall.
  5. **Prioritizing Showroom Trends Over Daily Wear:** High-gloss dark finishes look dramatic in photos, yet satin or matte finishes hide fingerprints and everyday smudges far better in active family kitchens.
