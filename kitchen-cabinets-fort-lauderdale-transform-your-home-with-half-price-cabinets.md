### **Why We Refuse to Sell Particle Board**

Many big-box stores lower their sticker prices by using compressed particle
board or low-grade MDF for cabinet boxes. Over time, heavy stone countertops,
loaded pantry items, and Florida humidity cause particle board to sag, pull
away from wall anchors, and swell when exposed to water leaks.

### **The Power of Solid Wood and Plywood**

We believe in doing things right the first time. Our product lines feature:

  * Solid wood door frames and face frames.
  * Cabinet boxes constructed from multi-ply, heavy-duty furniture-grade plywood.
  * Dovetail drawer construction for maximum structural joinery strength.
  * Soft-close door hinges and full-extension, soft-close drawer glides.
