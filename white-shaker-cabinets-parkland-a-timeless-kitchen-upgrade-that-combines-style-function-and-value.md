### **What Makes Shaker Cabinets Different?**

Shaker cabinets are distinguished by their five-piece door construction, which
typically features four surrounding frame components and a recessed center
panel.

Unlike ornate raised-panel doors, shaker cabinetry relies on balanced
proportions and simple detailing.

This design offers several advantages:

  * Clean surfaces that coordinate with different interior styles.
  * Recessed panels that add visual interest without excessive decoration.
  * Straightforward profiles that pair with modern or traditional hardware.
  * Versatile finishes that work with numerous countertop and flooring materials.
  * A design that feels equally appropriate in compact kitchens and expansive layouts.

We recommend considering shaker cabinets when homeowners want an attractive
kitchen without overwhelming it with decorative details.

## **Maintaining White Shaker Cabinets for Long-Term Beauty**

White cabinets offer a clean appearance, but regular care helps keep them
looking their best.

Fortunately, maintaining most finished shaker cabinet surfaces doesn't require
a complicated routine.

### **Simple Cleaning Practices**

We recommend removing everyday dust and light residue with a soft cloth and
cleaning products approved for the cabinet's finish.

Wipe spills promptly, especially around sinks and frequently used preparation
areas.

Avoid abrasive pads or harsh chemicals that could damage protective coatings.

Because cleaning requirements vary by cabinet manufacturer, always follow the
care instructions supplied with your specific cabinetry.

### **Protecting Cabinets From Moisture and Heat**

Kitchens naturally experience moisture, steam, and temperature changes.

Proper ventilation and prompt spill cleanup can help protect cabinet finishes
and surrounding materials.

We recommend paying particular attention to areas near dishwashers, sinks,
cooking surfaces, and other sources of steam.

Small preventive habits can help preserve the appearance and operation of your
cabinets over time.
