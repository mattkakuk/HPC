## **Maintenance Tips for Pristine White Cabinets**

Keeping white cabinets looking brand-new is easier than most people think. A
little consistent care keeps grease and grime at bay:

  1. **Daily Wipedowns:** Microfiber cloths and warm water mixed with mild dish soap are your best friends. Avoid harsh abrasive cleaners or scouring pads.
  2. **Tackle Cooking Splatters Early:** Wipe down cabinet doors near your stovetop right after cooking to prevent grease buildup.
  3. **Control Indoor Moisture:** Use your range hood exhaust fan while cooking to vent moisture and steam away from surrounding cabinetry.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**

### **1\. Are white kitchen cabinets hard to keep clean?**

Not at all! While dust and grease splatters show up more noticeably on light
surfaces than on dark wood, high-quality factory finishes make wiping away
messes quick and easy with just a microfiber cloth and mild soap.

### **2\. Will white cabinets turn yellow over time in South Florida 's
sunlight?**

Low-quality paints can turn yellow when exposed to UV light, but our cabinets
feature durable, UV-cured factory finishes designed to resist color shifts and
keep your whites looking bright for years.

### **3\. What countertop colors look best with white cabinetry?**

White cabinets pair beautifully with almost any countertop surface! Popular
choices include marble-look white quartz with subtle veining, dark granite for
dramatic contrast, or warm wood butcher block for an organic touch.

### **4\. Why is plywood construction better than particle board for
cabinets?**

Plywood consists of cross-layered wood veneers bonded together, offering
superior strength and moisture resistance. Unlike particle board, plywood does
not swell or sag when exposed to humidity.
